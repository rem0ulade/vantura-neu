Data paths you can explain

Grace Personal keeps chats on your Mac by default. External agents only receive what you send them. Optional hosted modes and Enterprise deployments (on-prem, private EU cloud, hybrid) are separate choices — see the privacy policy and Enterprise page.

Grace runs as a desktop workspace around your local server. The iOS companion connects over LAN or Tailscale for chats, tasks, approvals, status and push notifications without moving project content into a mandatory cloud.
