Before we talk

Straight answers.

Is media part of a data project?+

No. Media planning and campaign work are a separate offer. We only combine them if you ask for both.

Do we need a data or IT team?+

No. The usual client is an owner or operations lead in an SME. I connect the tools you already use.

Do we have to replace our systems?+

Usually not. Excel, accounting, CRM and existing exports stay. The new layer is the one reliable view.

How fast is a first result?+

A focused first version is often ready in two to four weeks. Scope and timeline are fixed before build.

What happens in the project check?+

Thirty minutes on the problem, the current files and the decision you need. You get a direct recommendation, including when I am the wrong fit.
